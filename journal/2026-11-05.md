- Checked J9 in the hardware manual: a **5-pin DIN** (part 303608), pinout 1 = +12, 2 = GND, 3 = +5, 4 = −5, 5 = GND (HWM p.37). J3 (audio) and J4 (tape) use the same DIN part, so the PSU plug fits all three sockets. Added this to hardware.md §14 and safety.md.

### PSU pin readings (unloaded, off board, probe ground on the PSU case)

The probe's physical switch is on **×1**. Pin 1 first read off-screen, then "116 V" with the scope set to ×10 (`20261105-144417_psu-pin1-offscreen-probe-x1.png`, `20261105-144426_psu-pin1-116V-probe-mismatch.png`). Set CH1 probe = 1 to match, and left the probe switch alone since ×1 is fine for DC.

| DIN pin | Expected (J9) | Measured VAVG | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | +12 V | **+11.60 V** | OK (11.4–12.6). Vpp 0.4 V at 5 V/div is quantisation, not ripple |
| 2 | GND | **+0.04 V** | OK, ground (connected to the PSU case) |
| 3 | +5 V | **+5.16 V** (0.5 V/div, offset −5 V) | OK (4.75–5.25, unloaded). Ripple, AC, 20 MHz BW limit, 10 µs/div: **148 mV p-p**, almost all of it SMPS switching spikes every ~10 µs (~50 kHz converter) with ringing; the baseline between spikes is ~20 mV. Normal for an unloaded AT PSU measured with a long ground lead. Judge it again under load. `20261105-144631_psu-pin3-5V-ripple-unloaded.png` |
| 4 | −5 V | **−7.03 V** (0.5 V/div) | **OUT OF RANGE** (−4.75 to −5.25). Steady, not noise. Probably poor regulation of the unloaded −5 V winding (AT PSUs regulate mainly on +5 V). Must re-measure under load. 4116 VBB operating range is about −4.5 to −5.7 V. |
| 5 | GND | **−0.07 V** | OK, ground |

**Result:** the plug wiring matches J9 exactly (1 = +12, 2 = GND, 3 = +5, 4 = −5, 5 = GND), and no pin carries −12 V. +12 V and +5 V are in range unloaded. **−5 V reads −7.03 V unloaded, out of range.** It must be re-checked under load before the board is connected. How much it matters depends on the DRAM type: 4116s use VBB, while on a 4164 board −5 V only feeds the 741 tape comparator and the RS-232 stage.

Scope left at CH1 DC, probe ×1, 5 V/div, 20 MHz BW limit on.

## Step 2: board photos (Phase 0 inventory)

Seven photos saved as `photos/20261105_*.jpg`, resized to 2500 px. **All originals contained GPS data**; stripped with exiftool and checked. The full inventory is in [docs/board.md](../docs/board.md). Key points:

- Original ITCI PCB **921442431 Rev A**, modified: **8× MMN 4164-2 in bank 3 and 8× КР565РУ6 in the video bank**. Both are single +5 V parts, so the **DRAM doesn't use −5 V**. The PSU's −7 V is now low priority (it feeds only the 741 and the RS-232 stage). Banks 0 and 2 are empty.

- ⚠ **The boot ROM looks reversed.** pag70 puts U89 pin 1 at the end with the empty socket positions, but the РФ5's triangle/bar marks are at the other end. ## Step 3: first power-up with the boot socket EMPTY

- The РФ5 was removed and stored. The PSU plug went into J9; the LCD is connected as before. The owner switched on before the scope was armed, so there's no power-up capture.
- **No smoke, all chips cool** after ~30 s.
- The scope dropped off USB at power-on (Errno 19) and recovered after an MCP `disconnect`/reconnect. Watch for this at every power switch.
- **LCD** (`captures/screen/20261105_noboot_lcd.jpg`, four frames from the owner's video in `..._rolling_frames.jpg`):
  - A light-grey border, and a picture area of alternating dark/light character-wide columns with fine horizontal line patterns. This is the classic **power-up DRAM content** of the video bank. The image is mono grey because the CoBra composite output is monochrome.
  - The pattern is static between frames.
  - **The picture rolls vertically** with a dark band (vertical blanking) moving through it. **Horizontal is stable**, so line sync is OK and the LCD isn't locking to the vertical sync.
- **Meaning:** the 14 MHz clock, the video counters, line sync, video-RAM reads (РУ6 bank) and the pixel/attribute/border path all work at least roughly. With no ROM the CPU executes FFh (RST 38h), so no meaningful writes are expected.
- This looks just like the original "blank characters" symptom. That fits the CPU never having run boot code then either (reversed ROM, or another early fault).
- **New issue: vertical roll.** Candidates: composite sync mixing (U88/U87, NVS), the REV02 sync-termination issue, or a frame rate the LCD dislikes. Check later on J1 pin 7 (composite) and J1 pin 8 (VS) with the scope: 50 Hz frame, 15.625 kHz line.
- +5 V under load: not measured yet; the probe was on the capacitor's GND end (−0.1 V).
- **+5 V under load** at C43 (100 µF beside the CPU, lead "A" = +, see `captures/screen/probe_point_C43.jpg`), ground clip on the PSU case: **VAVG 4.83 V** (in spec, but only 80 mV above the 4.75 V limit; it was 5.16 V unloaded, so ~0.33 V drops across the cable, connector and board). VMIN 4.26 / VMAX 5.30 V are spikes. The AC-coupled view shows ~0.9 V p-p of aliased spikes, which isn't meaningful with the ground clip on the PSU case. Next: move the ground clip to C43's other lead (B = GND) for a proper ripple measurement.
- **+5 V ripple under load**, measured properly (probe on C43 "A", ground clip on C43 "B" = board GND, AC, 20 MHz BW limit): **68 mV p-p at 1 µs/div**, mostly the probe noise floor. 88 mV p-p at 5 ms/div. **The +5 V rail is clean.** `captures/scope/screenshots/20261105-154044_5V-ripple-loaded-C43.png`
- Z80 (MMN 80CPU) orientation: the notch is at the end towards C43, so pin 1 is at the top left in `cpu_J9_reset.jpg`. Probe guide: `captures/screen/probe_point_Z80_pin6.jpg`.
