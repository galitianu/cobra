- Key new facts:
  - The original board uses **4116 DRAM**: +12 / +5 / −5 V, with −5 V first. J9 pinout: 1 = +12, 2 = GND, 3 = +5, 4 = −5, 5 = GND.
  - The boot socket U89 is 28-pin, with jumper SB selecting the 2716 VPP/A11 pin.
  - The FDC ports are F5h/FDh.
  - The 80K boot first touches bank #0 right after the screen clear.
- New top hypothesis: the РФ5's `LD D,83h` at 0000h only works if A powers up as FFh. Built `rom/custom/boot80k_rf5_fix0.bin` (MD5 `b7fb21f4770cc1499f488d1d7884a86a`): the РФ5 image with byte 0 changed 16→3E.
